# Code of conduct

Qubicl's community should be useful, direct, and safe to participate in.

Be respectful of different backgrounds and levels of experience. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Do not harass, threaten, demean, discriminate against, expose another person's private information, engage in sexualized conduct, or direct unwanted personal attention at another participant.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions consider context, severity, repeated behavior, and the safety of affected people.

Do not put sensitive conduct reports or another person's private information in
a public issue. Use GitHub's platform abuse-reporting flow when it applies. For
project moderation, contact a maintainer privately through a channel they list
on their GitHub profile. Maintainers will limit disclosure to what is necessary
to investigate and respond.
